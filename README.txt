JETWISE CAR JET SPRAY COMPARISON WEBSITE — SIX-BRAND EDITION

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open jetwise_car_jet_spray_website/index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. Use “6 Brand Deep Dive” for AGARO, TEXUM, STARQ, Shakti, Bosch and Dylect.
4. Use “Compare Machines” to search/filter models and open full details.

PAGES
- index.html — home
- products.html — searchable model directory and full details
- six-brands.html — focused comparison, winding claims, heat, auto-stop, pressure claims, feedback signals and market demand
- buying-guide.html — buying checklist
- market-analysis.html — demand, pressure and winding notes
- feedback.html — customer feedback form

PHOTOS / LOGOS
- Six-brand product photos load from brand/retailer image URLs and need internet access. Images may move or hotlink access may change. Each model links to a product listing.
- Brand-name tiles are text wordmarks, not exact official logo artwork.
- Other catalog models may still use illustrative SVGs; these are not actual product photos.

LIMITATIONS
- Prices/ratings are research snapshots, not guaranteed live offers.
- Review counts are not sales volumes or market share.
- No independent same-condition heat test or pressure-bench test for all six models was found; no definitive hottest/coolest or pressure accuracy winner is claimed.
- Advertised maximum bar can differ from rated working pressure. TEXUM TX-50 and STARQ ST-25 have differing figures across pages/graphics; verify exact SKU and manual.
- Winding/auto-stop is labelled as a claim or unconfirmed unless exact-model source evidence exists. Ask the manufacturer/authorised seller for written confirmation.
- Feedback is saved in local browser storage only; it is not uploaded online.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS site; no live price API or sales database.
