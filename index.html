DON'T BUY ON BAR ALONE

What actually matters?

01

Working pressure

Maximum advertised bar may differ from working pressure. Use a fan nozzle and keep distance from paint.

02

Water flow

L/min helps determine how quickly dirt and soap are rinsed away. Compare flow as well as pressure.

03

Hose & fittings

A 6–8 m hose is convenient for many home setups. Check couplers, replacement availability and leaks.

04

Switches & safety

Check the main power switch, trigger gun, thermal protection, warranty and safe electrical setup.
