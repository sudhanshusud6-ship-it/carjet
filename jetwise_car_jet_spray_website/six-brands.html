Heat: which brand runs hottest?

No defensible hottest-to-coolest ranking is available. No independent same-condition temperature test for all six models was found. Copper winding alone does not prove a machine runs cooler: pump design, motor efficiency, cooling, water supply, ambient temperature and duty cycle matter. Do not run a washer dry; follow the manual and stop if there is a burning smell, repeated thermal trips or abnormal noise.

Auto cut / auto-stop

Auto-stop usually means the pump stops when the spray-gun trigger is released. It is different from thermal overload protection. AGARO states automatic pump shut-off when the trigger is not engaged. STARQ ST-25 artwork claims auto power cutoff. Some Shakti S3 listings mention auto-stop. For TEXUM TX-50, Bosch Easy Aquatak 100 and Dylect Ultra Clean Plus, verify the exact model manual instead of assuming a feature from another variant.

Copper vs aluminium winding

Model-level evidence is uneven. STARQ ST-25 artwork claims 100% copper winding; Dylect Ultra Clean Plus brand material claims copper winding. For AGARO Supreme, TEXUM TX-50, Shakti S3/S3F and Bosch Easy Aquatak 100, this page does not treat winding as confirmed without a reliable exact-model manufacturer document. Ask the manufacturer/authorised seller for written confirmation. Do not open a mains-powered machine.

Is the advertised pressure actually achieved?

No independent pressure-bench test for all six models was found, so this site cannot certify that any machine sustains its advertised bar figure. TEXUM TX-50 illustrates the issue: the brand page advertises 185 bar, while a retailer page lists 145 bar. STARQ ST-25's product title says 150 bar while its infographic carries a higher maximum figure. Maximum pressure can differ from working pressure and measurement conditions. Ask for the rated working pressure and flow rate in the manual.

Which brand has the highest demand?

Available marketplace rating counts suggest high engagement for AGARO Supreme, TEXUM TX-50, STARQ ST-25 and Shakti S3. Rating counts are accumulated over time and are not units sold; sales ranking, regional availability and stock can differ. A reliable audited market-share table for these six brands was not found.

Customer feedback: what to inspect

  • AGARO: inspect low-star reports for leakage, gun/connector leaks and motor issues.
  • TEXUM: compare exact TX-50/TX-50D specifications, warranty and spare accessories.
  • STARQ: verify copper/auto-cut claims on the exact SKU and ask about service parts.
  • Shakti: S3 and S3F have different rating samples; review recent winding/build/service complaints separately.
  • Bosch: check exact Easy Aquatak variant, hose/accessories, manual and authorised service.
  • Dylect: verify the copper-winding claim, auto-stop/overload features and recent reviews.

These are review prompts, not claims that every unit has these faults. Feedback varies by seller, version and maintenance.
